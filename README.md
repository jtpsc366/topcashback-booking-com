Most cashback losses are preventable. Here's a checklist of the failure modes that show up repeatedly in user reviews, and how to dodge them:

- **Submit a missing cashback claim promptly.** If your transaction doesn't show up in pending within a week, file a claim through your TopCashback account. The longer you wait, the harder it is for the support team to recover the commission.
